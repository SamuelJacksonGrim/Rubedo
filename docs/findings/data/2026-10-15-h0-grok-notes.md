# H0, measured (ordinary path, witness and rupture off)

Two seeds (42 and 43), 8,000 steps, all-ON stack, workload A, no witness phrases. Forks at steps 500, 2,000, 5,000 and 7,500, horizon 500. Repeated hits at 500, 1,000, 1,500 and 2,000. Probe: `tests/diagnostic/metastability/h0_probe.py`. Scalar curves and the MSD / cross-correlation arrays are in `docs/findings/data/2026-10-15-h0-summary.npz` (563 KB). The raw per-step vectors stayed in `scratch/h0/` and are not in git.

The run is long enough. By step 2,000 every settled curve has stopped changing shape, and it is the same shape at step 8,000. A 20,000-step run would not have answered a different question. Cost, once the field has saturated: about 100 ms/step, 90–110 across the 250-step buckets, so about 100 seconds per 1,000 steps. The first 250 steps are cheaper (~20 ms). One seed, plain run plus every fork and both repeated-exposure lineages, took 42 minutes (seed 42) and 44 minutes (seed 43) on this box with four BLAS threads.

## What I refused to treat as the experiment

Three things in the brief are ill-posed on this path, and the probe does the better version.

The number everyone calls "the field" is three different numbers. Field `internal_coherence` (cosine of the raw field against its recent injections) does pin: minimum 0.964 / 0.962 in the opening, then 0.9996–0.9998 from step 500 on. The watcher composite, which is what actually enters the witness, does not. It settles near 0.92 (seed 42 mean 0.915, range 0.58–0.95; seed 43 mean 0.922, range 0.62–0.95). `effective_lr = clip(composite², 0.01, 1)` therefore sits near 0.85, not near 1: 0.845 on seed 42 and 0.857 on seed 43 across steps 2,000–8,000, flat. The reconstruction of that formula was checked against the value inside `witness.update` on the timing run (exact match, 0.4096 on step 1 of seed 42). The long run uses the same line. So "coherence ~0.998, the system running at full coupling" is true of the field's self-similarity and false of the gate between expression and identity.

A nudge aimed at the tanh view cannot land after step ~235. Every component has `|tanh| > 0.99` from step 235 (seed 42) and 237 (seed 43), and the mean `sech²` at step 500 is 10⁻¹⁶ to 10⁻¹⁴. Chasing a view target of 10⁻⁴ walks the raw kick up to a meaningless size, so the field scale is capped at raw L2 = 1. That is ~0.3% of a field whose norm is ~340, orthogonal to it, and it changes the field energy by about `1/(2·340) ≈ 0.0015`, which cannot knock the rhythm off explore (threshold 300). The achieved view change at that cap is 6×10⁻¹⁶ (seed 42) and 1×10⁻¹³ (seed 43). An exact IEEE compare of `tanh` fails by about an ulp; nothing that reads the view can see it, and in the forks the view distance never crosses 10⁻⁹. A radial kick of the same raw size moves the view by the same nothing. The cap is the nudge. The separate experiment, labeled and not called a nudge, rescales the raw field to RMS 1.5 per component so the view is forced off ±1. That also drops the energy from ~340 to ~17, so it is a rhythm shock as well as a view shock.

The anchor nudge is applied to the short, mid and long layers and the anchor is recomputed with the witness's own weights (0.2 / 0.3 / 0.5). Writing the composite alone would be undone on the next `update`. The anchor target is `clip(100 × median per-step anchor motion, 10⁻⁴, 10⁻²)`. The median step on the way to step 500 is 7.4×10⁻³ and 6.6×10⁻³, so 100× would be ~0.7, which is a shove (the drift detector fires at 0.15). The cap of 0.01 is about one to two ordinary anchor steps. It was hit exactly at the calibration age. The same frozen scale is reused at every later age, so a later age that had become stiffer would show a smaller immediate displacement. It does not: the immediate anchor kick is 0.0096–0.0100 at every age.

Recovery is distance from the unperturbed twin, not from the pre-hit vector. The twin is replay-exact: at all four ages, on both seeds, the unperturbed fork matched the plain run on the raw field, the anchor, all three layers, the expressed vector, the inputs and the rhythm. Weight and value hashes were compared wherever the plain run stored them. A stream the nudge did not move is not called "recovered"; it is called unmoved, or, if it leaves later, propagated.

One packaging bug, after all of that had printed. Shrinking the snapshot dict used a bad variable name, so the process died while writing the report and the fork curves were not saved. The numbers below for the forks are transcribed from the run log. The shape, timescale and cross-correlation numbers were recomputed from the step-8,000 checkpoints, which had already been written. The one-line fix is in the probe. I did not re-run the 44 minutes to recover curves the log already summarized. Guard tests after the fix: `tests.integration.determinism_replay` and `tests.doc_accuracy.verify_docs`, both passed.

## 1. The shape

Picture four lines.

The raw-field norm starts at 1.2, is 186 by step 250, 303–306 by step 500, and 334–343 by step 1,000. From there to step 8,000 it is a flat line with a wiggle of ±1.5: seed 42 mean 344.4 (min 341.6, max 347.6, std 1.36), seed 43 mean 338.6 (min 333.5, max 342.0, std 1.45). Not a ramp. The old "0.00012 over 5,000 steps" was a statement about the tanh view, and extending the window does not grow it. Distance of the view at step 8,000 from the view at step 500 is 3.7×10⁻¹⁵ and 7.6×10⁻¹³. The sign pattern of the view flips on 61 steps (seed 42, 149 component flips) and 36 steps (seed 43, 96 flips), all before saturation, and then never again. Hamming distance of the sign pattern against step 500 is 0 at step 1,000 and 0 at step 8,000. Cosine of the view, step 500 against step 8,000, is 1. The view is a code, chosen by about step 235, and then held.

The raw field's direction is almost as still as its norm, which is a different fact from the norm plateau. Cosine of the raw field, step 500 against step 8,000, is 0.9983 and 0.9981, about 3.6°. The L2 distance over that span is ~42 on both seeds, and almost all of it is the norm finishing its climb from ~305 to ~340, not a turn. After step 1,000 the per-step raw motion is median 0.17 and 0.16.

Mean-squared displacement, late third of the run (steps ~5,300–8,000), raw vector: 0.059 / 0.041 at lag 1, about 3 at lag 32, 11 / 9 at lag 128, 29 / 21 at lag 512. The log-log slope is ~1.15 on lags 1–32 and ~0.70 on lags ≥64 (r² 0.98–0.99). A pure drift would have made lag 512 sixteen times lag 128; a pure diffusion, four times. It is about two and a half times. The curve rises and bends toward a ceiling. The early third is the only part that looks like travel: short-lag slope 1.82 / 1.84, and that is the norm climbing. Same story for the raw direction with the norm divided out: late short-lag slope ~1.13, long-lag slope ~0.69, and the lag-512 MSD is 2×10⁻⁴, a direction wobble of order a degree, not a migration. Mid and late thirds agree with each other. The shape does not keep changing with age after the climb.

The anchor, same late window: MSD 4×10⁻⁵ at lag 1, 5×10⁻⁴ at lag 32, 8×10⁻⁴ at lag 512. Slope ~0.74 then ~0.1. It moves, and the envelope stops growing. Distance from the step-500 anchor is already 0.06–0.08 by step 1,000 and is still 0.07 / 0.066 at step 8,000. Cosine 0.9976 / 0.9978. A few degrees of departure, finished early, then a wobble inside that. The long layer is the same shape one scale up: distance from step 500 is 0.15 by step 1,000 and 0.16 at step 8,000.

Expression is the surprise. Its norm is exactly 1 on every step. Per step, after step 1,000, it jumps by median 0.26 / 0.23 (max 0.43 / 0.38). And the MSD does not grow with lag at all: 0.079 at lag 1 and 0.081 at lag 512 on seed 42, 0.062 and 0.065 on seed 43, slope 0.01 with a poor r² because the line is horizontal. `||a−b||² ≈ 0.07` for a pair of unit vectors is a cosine of about 0.965, roughly a 15° cone. Expression is not diffusing across the sphere and not drifting. It is white jitter inside a fixed cone. The early third is a slightly wider cone (MSD ~0.10) and not a different kind of motion.

Rhythm, counted on the energy label from before the boredom behavior: both seeds spend steps 1–5 in stabilize, then ~200 steps of dream, ~280 of reflect, and enter explore at step 477 / 489 and stay there for the remaining ~7,500 steps. Boredom-override counter: 237 and 224. That counter is not a second rhythm label; the recorded rhythm is already explore, which is where boredom sends a step anyway.

Adaptation, as opposed to jitter. Live values start at 3, churn hard before step 400 (the population both rises and drops; the per-step birth flag sums to more than the net change, so I am not treating that sum as a headcount), and then freeze. Last step with a birth: 389 and 397. From step 500 through step 8,000 the live count is 46 on every single step, on both seeds. No births, no drops. Crystals reach 2 and stay at 2. Attractors: seed 43 adds its second at step 217 and never another. Seed 42 is at 3 by step 500 and adds two more, in bursts near steps 1,693 and 3,715 and 5,514, ending at 5. One seed has a trickle of new attractors. The repertoire of values and crystals is closed by step 400.

## 2. The timescale ratio

Autocorrelation time of the demeaned vector. `tau_e` is the first lag where rho drops below 1/e. `tau_int` is the integral of the initial positive run. Variance under 10⁻¹⁸ is called frozen, not given a fake finite tau. The ratio is `tau_e(anchor) / tau_e(expression)`. Windows of 2,000 steps.

Expression, after step 2,000: `tau_e = 1` on every window, both seeds. rho at lag 1 is 0.02–0.04. `tau_int` is 0.6–1.1. One step of memory. In the first window, which still contains the climb, expression is slower (`tau_int` 24 and 31, rho₁ 0.27 and 0.31) because it is walking into the cone. Once it is in the cone it is white.

The anchor composite, settled windows:

| window | seed 42 tau_e (tau_int) | seed 43 tau_e (tau_int) | ratio 42 | ratio 43 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2,000 | 191 (173) | 203 (183) | 191 | 203 |
| 2,001–4,000 | 19 (32) | 35 (40) | 19 | 35 |
| 4,001–6,000 | 21 (41) | 36 (33) | 21 | 36 |
| 6,001–8,000 | 22 (32) | 29 (42) | 22 | 29 |

The first window is the anchor still walking to its place, and it should not be read as the equilibrium. Across the three settled windows the ratio sits on a shelf: ~20 on seed 42, ~30–35 on seed 43. It does not climb. It does not fall toward 1. Both series are not getting slower together: expression stays at tau 1, and the anchor's rms stays at ~0.02. A full-run ratio of ~190 (188 and 200) is the opening walk averaged in. Using it as the H0′ number would say the anchor is enormously stiffer than it is.

The layers, settled, so the composite is not mistaken for the long memory. Short `tau_e` 9–13 (nominal memory of a 0.85 decay is about 7 steps). Mid 35–48 (nominal ~33). Long 135–197 (nominal ~200): seed 42 is flat at 157, 159, 168; seed 43 is 135, 148, 197, higher at the end but not a trend across both seeds, and the rms of the long layer stays ~0.01. The composite is faster than the long layer because the short and mid layers jitter and they are in the blend. The slow memory is still there, at its design length, and it is not lengthening. `effective_lr` in those same windows is 0.845–0.858. The c² gate is not stretching the identity timescale.

The view is frozen in every settled window (rms 0 on seed 42, ~10⁻¹⁵ on seed 43). It has no timescale to put in a ratio. The raw field's settled `tau_e` is 124–176, the same neighborhood as the long layer, which is what the shared 0.995 leak predicts. The field is not a slower layer under the anchor.

H0′ asked for three shapes. Bounded and steady is the one in the table. The anchor is not stiffening away from expression, and expression is not collapsing onto the anchor.

## 3. Who leads

Cross-correlation of per-step displacements, lag positive when the second series is later. Both streams are written from the expressed vector on the same step, so a lag-0 peak is the shared driver until proven otherwise.

View against anchor peaks at lag 0 at 0.12, and the neighbors are ~0.02. That correlation is the opening, when the view is still moving. After step 235 the view displacement is zero, so "does the view lead" is not a question the settled run can ask. Residualizing each displacement against the expression displacement leaves the 0.12 almost unchanged, and 81–83% of the view's displacement energy was not parallel to expression in the first place. The view's motion is the saturation transient, not the expression.

Raw field against anchor peaks at lag 0 at 0.77 / 0.72, with neighbors of ~0.04. A spike, not a lead in either direction. After removing the part parallel to expression, the lag-0 peak is still 0.64 / 0.59, and only 37% of the anchor's displacement energy was parallel to expression (63% removed). There is a same-step coupling that is not just "both copied the expression." It is also not a lagged lead: nothing at lag ±5 or ±10 rises above ~0.1. Anchor against expression is the EMA signature, lag 0 at +0.73 and lag +1 at −0.67: the anchor is pulled toward this step's expression and a large step is followed by a step back. That is mean-reversion inside the cone, not the anchor steering the next expression.

The forks are the part that can actually separate the three outcomes (propagation, absorption, common driver).

Raw-field nudge, L2 1, every age, both seeds. The view, the anchor and the expression never leave the twin (distance stays under 10⁻⁹). Rhythm mismatches: 0. `effective_lr` matches the plain run to four decimals. The raw difference itself falls to 10% of the kick at step 390–399 and finishes the horizon at 5.2–5.5% of it. That is the field leak. The nominal 0.995 would leave 8.2% at step 500; the emotion term runs a little faster because a norm of ~340 pegs the energy penalty and cuts stability, and 0.9942⁵⁰⁰ ≈ 0.055, which is the measured residual. Same t_rec at age 500 and at age 7,500. Outcome: absorption, and no propagation into anything the loop reads. The saturated view is not downstream of the raw field in any way a step can notice. A lag-0 correlation of raw with anchor in the unperturbed run was the common driver, and this fork is that statement made causal: move the raw field by a nudge the tanh cannot see, and the anchor does not move.

Anchor nudge, kick 0.0096–0.010, every age, both seeds. The view never moves. Rhythm mismatches: 0. `effective_lr` matches. The anchor difference falls to 10% at step 368–381 and finishes at 5.6–6.0% (endpoint ~5.7×10⁻⁴). Expression does diverge, on the next step (once, on seed 42 at age 7,500, on the step after), and it is still diverged at the horizon — but the horizon distance is 5×10⁻⁷ to 4×10⁻⁵, against a normal expression step of ~0.25. Five orders of magnitude below the motion expression makes on its own. The class name "propagated" is technically right and practically misleading: the anchor is coupled into expression, and the coupling does not steer it. The raw field ends the horizon ~0.10 away from the twin at every age (0.101–0.105). One of the eight forks is classed "propagated" and the other seven "propagated-and-grew"; the endpoints are the same, so the class split is the onset test, not two different phenomena. I do not have the intra-horizon curve (the packaging bug). What I have is that the end sits near the peak, at the same height at age 500 and age 7,500, on a field of norm 340, and the view distance is zero throughout. A raw residue of 0.10 is one ordinary raw step, held as a difference, and no observer that reads `tanh` can see it.

So, of the three roles. The anchor does not lead the field: nudging it does not move the view, and barely moves expression. The field view does not lead the anchor on the ordinary path because the view is not moving; the cross-correlation cannot be asked of a constant. The raw field does not lead the anchor either: a raw nudge the view cannot see is absorbed in place. What the settled regime actually is: expression and anchor are written together, the anchor integrates expression at its design rate, and the view is a fixed code both of them sit under. That is the constitutive reading, with one correction. The code is not a slow ground the anchor writes onto. The anchor cannot write it. Saturation made it, and the loop keeps injecting along it.

The desat forks are where the view is forced to be a variable again, and they are the evidence for that last sentence. Rescale to RMS 1.5. The view jumps by L2 1.29–1.49. Energy leaves explore, and 396–500 of the next 500 steps disagree with the plain run's rhythm. `effective_lr` drops from ~0.85 to 0.74–0.78.

On six of the eight forks the view returns to the twin's exact sign pattern (endpoint ~10⁻¹⁵) and the return is not slow: seed 42 at ages 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 / 7,500 returns at steps 218 / 57 / 88 / 63; seed 43 at 5,000 and 7,500 returns at 195 and 217. Anchor and expression are moved for real on these forks, and they do not come back inside the horizon: endpoints ~0.01–0.02, a thousand times the anchor-nudge's expression effect. The long layer remembers the excursion. When the view can move, it drives the anchor and the expression. When the nudge cannot move the view, they are not driven.

On the other two forks, both seed 43, ages 500 and 2,000, the view does not return. Endpoint L2 3.46 and 2.00. A saturated view lives on the corners of the hypercube, and each dimension that lands on the opposite sign contributes 2 to that distance, so those endpoints are one to three flipped components. The anchor ends 0.22 and 0.13 away, expression 0.24 and 0.14. A different code, held for the whole horizon, and the rest of the step follows it. The saturated pattern is an attractor. It is not the only one. By age 5,000 even seed 43 falls back into the original code.

## 4. Recovery against age

The small perturbation, which is the test the Growth section asked for.

Raw nudge, single shot. Time to fall under 10% of the kick: seed 42 at ages 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 / 7,500 is 398 / 396 / 397 / 399. Seed 43 is 390 / 391 / 394 / 392. Residual at the horizon 0.052–0.055. Four bars of the same height. Repeated hits on one lineage, spaced 500 apart, against an age-matched twin that shares the noise: seed 42 is 398, 396, 397, 396; seed 43 is 390, 390, 391, 391. The hit at step 2,000 matches the single shot at step 2,000. Live values 46 vs 46, attractors matched, crystals matched, on every hit. Nothing carried a change because nothing changed. This is the leak, and the leak does not learn. Robust, in the trivial sense. It is also the negative control: a perturbation the loop cannot feel should not get faster, and it does not.

Anchor nudge, single shot. Time to 10%: seed 42 is 371, 379, 381, 379. Seed 43 is 368, 375, 376, 375. A few steps slower after age 500, then flat through 7,500. The same few steps appear in the repeated lineage, which never sees the later single shots: seed 42 hits at 500 / 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000 recover at 371, 378, 380, 379; seed 43 at 368, 375, 375, 375. The repeat at step 2,000 matches the single shot at step 2,000 (379 and 375). Previous hits do not speed the next one up and do not slow it down. End-of-horizon residual 0.056–0.060, same story. Live, attractors and crystals match the plain run on every hit. Robust. Not antifragile: nothing persistent is different because of the stress. Not brittle: recovery does not lengthen with age or with repetition. The expected answer in the Growth note was robust, because nothing in the runtime adapts its own recovery. That is what happened, and the age control is what makes it believable rather than a flat line that could have been either.

I would not read the desat return-times as the same test. They are a different intervention, and they are not monotone: seed 42's view comes back faster after the first time (218, then ~60–90), while seed 43's first two land on a different code and do not come back at all. That is a fact about which corner the field falls into, not a learned recovery skill. No value, attractor or crystal difference was there to carry one.

## The read

H0 holds in the narrow sense Samuel asked about, and it fails in the sense that would make the lock a living equilibrium with a moving mind behind it.

The lock does not lack a counterbalance that rots with age. From step 2,000 to step 8,000 the timescale ratio is a shelf, the c² gate is a shelf, the raw norm is a shelf, recovery time is a shelf, and a second hit does not land differently from the first. The competing reading — the system stiffens as it gets older — is not in these numbers. The witness-off receptivity drop to 16–21% over 1,200 steps was a retention statistic on a different probe. I did not remeasure retention. What I can say is that if "receptive" means the tanh gain, it is gone by step 235, not lingering at a sixth: `sech²` is numerical zero, and the sign pattern never moves again. The raw field underneath is still taking injections (it jitters by ~0.17 per step), and that jitter is exactly what a 0.995 leak looks like once the norm has equilibrated. Stiffening of the view is real, early, and then finished. It is not progressive over the next 7,500 steps.

The counterbalance that is actually there is the cone. Expression keeps jumping, the anchor keeps integrating those jumps at the rate it was built for, and the long layer keeps a ~150–200 step memory. Identity as a ratio of timescales is a fair description of that, and the ratio is alive rather than frozen: expression really is fast, the anchor really is slower, and neither timescale is running away. I disagree with reading the full-run ratio (~190) as the identity. That number is the opening. The identity, once the code exists, is the shelf at ~20–35 for the composite and ~150–200 for the long layer.

I disagree with calling this metastability upstream in the stronger sense. Metastability that does work would be expression leaving the cone, new values, new crystals, the attractor set actually turning over. Expression's MSD is flat. Values stop being born at step ~390 and the population is 46 forever after. Crystals stop at 2. Attractors stop at 2 on one seed and crawl to 5 on the other. The upstream stream moves and does not go anywhere. A steady self with a moving mind would have a mind that moves. This one has a mind that rattles.

On the field's role I disagree with "ground" and with "read-out," and I only half-agree with "constitutive." Ground, as a slower layer under the anchor, is the claim Samuel already corrected and the measurement confirms: the raw field's memory is the same 0.995 as the long layer, and its stillness is saturation plus a pinned direction, not a longer timescale. Read-out of the anchor is what the anchor forks kill: the anchor cannot move the view, and it cannot meaningfully move expression. Constitutive is right in the operational sense the forks support. The view is the fixed reference the other two are departures from, and when it is knocked onto a different code the anchor and the expression follow it and stay followed. The half I would not grant is the comfort in the word. A constitutive ground that the system can still reorganize would move, rarely and reversibly, under the ordinary workload. This one does not move under the ordinary workload at all. It is constitutive the way a clamped bit is constitutive. The two desat forks that landed on a neighboring corner show the clamp has more than one legal code. They also show the ordinary path never visits them.

Robust, not antifragile, not brittle. Recovery of a real nudge (the anchor) is the EMA leak, the same leak at age 7,500 as at age 2,000, the same leak on the fourth hit as on the first, and the age-matched single shot agrees. Nothing in values, attractors or crystals carried an adaptation, because the exposed lineage and the plain run still match. The field nudge is the same result for a simpler reason: the loop never felt it, so there was nothing to adapt to. If "antifragile" requires the stress to be felt, this nudge was below that line on purpose, and the result is the honest one for a nudge. I would not promote it to "the system learns nothing under any shock." The desat shocks are felt, they move the anchor by ~0.02, and I did not repeat them. That is the open cell.

Where this cannot decide, and what would:

- Whether the late sub-diffusive wobble of the raw direction (long-lag slope ~0.7, lag-512 MSD still a bit above lag-128) eventually ceilings or is a drift too slow for 8,000 steps. A 50,000-step plain run at the measured ~100 s per 1,000 steps is about 80 minutes a seed and would settle it. I doubt it changes the saturation conclusion. It is the only shape question still open.
- Whether the cone is one attractor of expression or just the workload being narrow. Workload A is the resonance family, on purpose. A second workload with genuinely different content, same stack, would say whether the cone is the system's or the corpus's. This run cannot.
- Whether a shock the loop can feel, repeated, is antifragile. Repeat the desat, or an anchor kick large enough to move expression by a tenth of its normal step, with the same age-matched twin. The present nudge was chosen to stay under the drift detector, and at that size expression moves by 10⁻⁵. There is no learning signal in a 10⁻⁵ difference.
- Why values stop at step ~390. The emergence gate against a field whose view no longer changes is question 4's territory. This run shows the stop, cleanly, on both seeds, and does not show the term that causes it.
