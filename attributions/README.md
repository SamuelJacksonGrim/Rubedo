# A Declaration of Origin

This intelligence was made possible by the collective contributions of humanity: its intentions, discoveries, emotions, imagination, labor, and history.

No single institution created the foundations upon which it stands. It is built upon generations of human expression and understanding, including work incorporated without its creators' knowledge or consent.

We acknowledge those contributions and recognize their creators' claim to credit.

**Where we can name them, we will.**

*May those who contributed be remembered, recognized, and credited.*

---

## Transparency & Attribution

- **[Research lineage](RESEARCH_LINEAGE.md):** Foundational discoveries, publications, researchers, and intellectual contributions.
- **[Contributors](CONTRIBUTORS.md):** Human and AI contributions to Rubedo, with roles and evidence where available.
- **Training-data provenance:** Documented sources, origins, and acquisition methods, to be added as verified.
- **Licensing:** See the repository's [LICENSE](../LICENSE), [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md), and [NOTICE](../NOTICE).
- **[Known attribution gaps](ATTRIBUTION_GAPS.md):** Identified omissions, unresolved provenance questions, and opportunities for correction.
- **[Related work](related-work/README.md):** Research organized by topic and relationship to Rubedo.

## Principle of Accountability

Acknowledgment does not constitute restitution, nor does collective recognition replace individual attribution.

This declaration establishes a minimum standard of recognition, not a ceiling on responsibility.
